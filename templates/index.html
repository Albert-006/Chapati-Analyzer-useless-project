CHAPATI ANALYZER™ LABORATORY DOSSIER

"We took something that should be judged by someone's grandmother and turned it into a computer-vision problem."

1. Computer Vision Pipeline

Chapati Analyzer™ operates strictly on classical digital image processing without black-box neural networks. The pipeline performs adaptive color thresholding in HSV and CIE Lab space, followed by Otsu binarization and morphological contour extraction.

2. Mathematical Formulations

  • Circularity Index: Circularity = 4πA / P², where A is contour area and P is perimeter.
  • Equivalent Diameter: D = 2 × √(A / π).
  • Radial Sampling: Evaluates 360 angular rays r(θ) from the centroid to compute the coefficient of variation CV = σ / μ.
  • Rotational Symmetry: Cross-evaluates radial symmetry |r(θ) - r(θ + 180°)|.

3. Browning & Texture Quantification

Toasting patterns are analyzed across 5 concentric radial zones (Center, Inner, Middle, Outer, Edge) by measuring relative lightness drops from dough baseline, computing the burn ratio and patch morphology. Surface texture is extracted using eroded Laplacian variance and local variance.

4. Future Stage 2 Architecture

The system stores a normalized 360-bin scale-invariant shape signature for every specimen, designed to support the planned Chapati Shape Genome™, hierarchical clustering, and evolutionary lineage trees.
